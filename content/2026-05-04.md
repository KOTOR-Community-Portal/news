# New city, who dis?

I bid you all Dark Greetings.

We have just completed a major overhaul of the KOTOR Community Portal. I am happy to report that things are finally looking how I imagined when we first decided to migrate content from the r/kotor subreddit to a standalone website hosted by Neocities—it will be 3 years in June.

This update involved a total rewrite of the website's build system to lay the foundation for some more exciting features we have planned. Since so much had to be redone anyway, I took the opportunity to revisit the design of the website, with a focus on improving user experience:

- Primary navigation is located in the header and social media links are located in the footer, where they belong.
- Secondary navigation, like the table of contents, is now sticky on wider screens, so you can scroll through it separately from the main content.
- Site settings are accessible from a hamburger menu which is styled after the in-game computer terminal interface.
- Sections of the main content may be collapsed. This should prove helpful on those longer pages like the Mod Builds.
- Longer pages also include a floating "Back to Top" button in the bottom-right corner.
- The "Quick Info" section is located closer to the top of the page, where it can be accessed more quickly.

Behind the scenes, web pages were updated to better conform to semantic HTML and web accessibility guidelines. This will enable more users to access the website, particularly those who rely on assistive technology. As an added bonus, this supposedly also helps with search engine optimization.

Last but not least, we've filled the website with ads—but they're silly in-universe ads. I hope you enjoy them. There will be more to come.
