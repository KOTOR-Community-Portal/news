# May the Fifth Be With You

Yesterday's update went well... mostly! Unfortunately, there are a couple of known issues I would like to bring to your attention.

- If you encounter a page which loads but looks completely broken, try clearing your browser's cache. CTRL+F5 will clear the cache and reload the current page on Firefox and Chrome (and other Chromium-based browsers).
- There are issues with the settings modal on mobile devices. It should be be functional in Chrome on both Android and iOS when the phone is in landscape mode, but otherwise it has various problems. There are also various minor style issues which are only present on mobile browsers. I will be looking into a method of testing on physical devices before the next major update so we'll be able to spot issues like this before going live in future.

If you experience any other issues or if you have other suggestions for the website, you can let us know in the #yes_general channel of our [Discord server]().
