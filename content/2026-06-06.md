# New Delhi, New Pages

Yesterday, the KOTOR Community Portal was offline for several hours due to an outage affecting the entire neocities.org domain. The cause of the outage was not technical, and actually quite ridiculous, as explained in [a Bluesky post from the official Neocities account](https://bsky.app/profile/neocities.org/post/3mnkqgxostk2k):

> "Early this morning, we received an email from @namecheap.com that the domain neocities.org was being suspended because of a court order from New Delhi, India (a jurisdiction we don't operate in), which was for an unrelated court order about a sports streaming site on a different domain."

This outage could not have been predicted by anyone and is extremely unlikely to happen again; however, it highlights the threat of lost media and the volatility of the modern internet which makes it especially vulnerable. To reduce the impact of future disruptions, I have created a backup of the KOTOR Community Portal on GitHub Pages, available at [kotor-community-portal.github.io](https://kotor-community-portal.github.io/). This was on the to-do list before, but yesterday we were given a more compelling reason to prioritize it.

The backup is functionally identical to the main website and future updates will be deployed to both at the same time. While I encourage you to continue using the [kotor.neocities.org](/) address, this backup will provide an extra safety net in the event of future Neocities outages.
